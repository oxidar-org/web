## The Talks

### 1. Secret Recovery without a Centralized Custodian

Facundo La Rocca will tell us how DeRec solves the problem of the lost *seed phrase*, with Rust at its core. In this presentation, he'll explain how decentralized secret recovery works without relying on custodians or intermediaries.

### 2. What if Rust had the ergonomics of Spring or NextJS?

Fernando Pastorelli will present a talk about a framework built on top of Axum that offers macros, powerful abstractions, shareable configuration, and a plugin system. The project aims to achieve fast *ramp-up* without sacrificing performance, guaranteeing very low *cold starts*.
