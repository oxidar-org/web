## Why embeddings?

Embeddings are behind a good part of what we now call artificial intelligence: search engines that understand what you meant and not just what you typed, recommendations, document clustering, systems that give context to a language model. The idea is simple and powerful: represent texts (or images, or anything else) as vectors of numbers, so that similar things end up close together.

And Rust is a great language to work with them: fast, predictable with memory, and with an ecosystem for machine learning that keeps getting better. In this workshop we're going to bring the two together.

## Two venues, one day

We're running this event together with two local teams who will lead each venue:

- **Bariloche**: run by a team from **INVAP**. Location to be confirmed.
- **Santa Fe**: run by the **Universidad Nacional del Litoral**. Building to be confirmed.

From **Oxidar.org** we coordinate the event and provide online support to both venues throughout the day.

## What will the day look like?

The format is similar to the [hackathon we held in Buenos Aires](/snakear-recap/):

1. **Welcome**: introductions and how the day will work.
2. **Learning session** (about an hour): what embeddings are, how they work, and what they're used for.
3. **Hands-on**: we'll work with a **starter kit prepared by INVAP**, designed for those who are just getting started with embeddings.
4. **The classic Oxidar quiz**: because there's no event without a Rust trivia.

> **Note:** you don't need to know anything about embeddings or machine learning. The workshop is designed so that anyone who programs in Rust (or wants to start) walks away with something new.

## What if I'm not in Bariloche or Santa Fe?

You can take part too! We'll stream on **YouTube**, and you'll be able to follow the workshop at your own pace with an **emulator** that we'll share, in a self-guided format. Questions and conversation will happen in our [Telegram](https://t.me/+7PgAQVPclxIzOGQ0).
